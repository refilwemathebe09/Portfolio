About Me

A quantity surveyor learning to think in systems.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Construction Studies and a Bachelor of Science Honours in Quantity Surveying from the University of the Witwatersrand. That foundation gave me a working understanding of cost management, procurement, financial reporting, project coordination, commercial management, stakeholder engagement and risk identification the everyday mechanics of getting a project built on budget.

I'm not transitioning away from construction into technology. I'm building a bridge between the two combining practical industry knowledge with data and analytical capabilities I'm developing in parallel, including Python, SQL, data analysis and the fundamentals of artificial intelligence.

My long-term vision is to build practical digital tools and intelligent systems that solve real problems for quantity surveyors, construction companies, engineering firms, mining companies, financial institutions and other project-driven businesses.
